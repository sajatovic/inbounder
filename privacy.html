Inbounder Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Inbounder ("we", "our", or "the App") handles your information.

We prioritize user privacy by strictly limiting the data we handle and partnering only with trusted services to support the App.

1. Account & Personal Information

  • No Account Creation: You are not required to create an account, register, or provide direct personal identifiers (such as your name, email, or telephone number) to use the App.
  • No Direct Sale of Data: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to data brokers or unrelated third parties.

2. Location Information

To calculate and display overhead aircraft and transit relative to your position, the App requires access to geographic coordinates:

  • On-Device Processing: When Location Services are enabled, your device’s latitude and longitude are utilized strictly to calculate distance, bearing, and look-up elevation angles.
  • No Location Logging: Your location data is processed ephemerally. We do not maintain historical logs of your movements, store your GPS coordinates in a persistent user profile, or associate your location with an identifiable device profile.
  • Location Presets: Any custom saved locations (such as presets for home or work) are stored locally on your device and are never transmitted to an external analytics database.

3. Push Notifications

If you choose to receive alerts when a vehicle enters your proximity:

  • Device Tokens: The App requests an anonymous Apple Push Notification service (APNs) device token generated by your operating system.
  • Scope of Use: This token is used solely to route overhead notifications to your device. It contains no personal information and is not linked to your identity.

4. Advertising and Tracking (Google AdMob)

To support ongoing development and keep the App accessible, we display advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

  • Data Collected by AdMob: When ads are served, Google AdMob may automatically collect certain device information, including your device's advertising identifier (Apple IDFA or Google Advertising ID), IP address, general coarse location (derived from IP address), device model, operating system version, and ad interaction metrics.
  • App Tracking Transparency (ATT): On iOS 14.5 and later, we ask for your permission before accessing your Advertising Identifier (IDFA) to deliver personalized ads or track your activity across apps and websites owned by other companies. If you decline ("Ask App not to Track"), your IDFA will not be used, and you will receive non-personalized, contextual advertisements instead.
  • Ad Personalization & Opt-Out: You can manage or reset your advertising identifier and opt out of interest-based ads anytime through your device settings:
    • iOS: Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking or Apple Advertising.
    • Android: Go to Settings > Privacy > Ads.
  • Google Privacy Policy: For more details on how Google processes ad data, please review the Google Advertising & Privacy Policy.

5. Telemetry Feeds & Third-Party APIs

The App queries publicly available flight and transit telemetry feeds (such as ADS-B aggregators) to retrieve real-time vehicle positions. These queries request data for geographic bounding boxes and do not transmit your identity, advertising ID, or personal data to external telemetry providers.

6. Children’s Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect, solicit, or track personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe an underage child has provided personal information through our advertising partner or support channels, please contact us immediately to remove it.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
