General Overview & Architecture

What is Intellirush Inbounder?

Intellirush Inbounder is a native iOS, iPadOS, and macOS real-time overhead flight tracking and spatial radar application. It monitors the airspace directly above you or any designated map center, alerting you the moment an aircraft enters your proximity radius or passes directly overhead.

Featuring live vector radar, airline operator identification, aircraft model telemetry, and an intuitive Horizon Look-Up Perspective Gauge, Inbounder lets aviation enthusiasts and spotters identify flights overhead in seconds.

For more details, product updates, and platform documentation, visit www.intellirush.com.

How does the tracking engine work? (Intellirush Backend)

Inbounder connects directly to the high-performance Intellirush Cloud Backend. Built and operated by Intellirush, our cloud engine continuously ingests and aggregates high-throughput live ADS-B, transponder, and radar telemetry streams in real time.

  • Zero Configuration Required: No API keys, credentials, or third-party accounts needed. Tracking is immediate upon opening the app.
  • Sub-Second Precision: High-frequency telemetry updates with minimal latency.
  • Server-Side Spatial Indexing: Aircraft bounding boxes and proximity thresholds are computed server-side, preserving your device's battery life and cellular data.
  • 24/7 Cloud Geofence Monitoring: The Intellirush cloud backend continuously tracks your geofence and dispatches background Apple Push Notifications (APNs) even when Inbounder is closed.
💡
High-Availability Infrastructure: The dedicated Intellirush Cloud Backend provides reliable, continuous flight tracking with zero rate limiting and no user credentials required. Learn more at www.intellirush.com.
What types of aircraft can I track?

Intellirush Inbounder tracks all transponder-equipped aircraft in active airspace, including:

  • Commercial Airliners: Major passenger carriers worldwide with airline logos and origin/destination city pairs.
  • Cargo & Logistics: Dedicated airfreight carriers (FedEx, UPS, DHL, Atlas Air, etc.).
  • Regional & Commuter: Embraer E-Jets, Bombardier CRJ, and ATR turboprops.
  • General Aviation: Private jets, business aircraft, and single/twin-engine propeller planes.
  • Helicopters & Specialized Aircraft: Civil rotorcraft and law enforcement/emergency air medical flights.

Radar, Telemetry & Horizon Gauge

What is the Horizon Look-Up Perspective Gauge?

The Horizon Look-Up Perspective Gauge solves the age-old question: "Where exactly do I look in the sky?"

By calculating the aircraft's altitude, your elevation, and the exact slant range distance between you and the aircraft, Inbounder plots an elevation arc from 0° (Horizon) to 90° (Directly Overhead / Zenith) with practical, plain-English observation guidance:

  • 0° – 15°: "Look straight towards the horizon"
  • 15° – 45°: "Look slightly above treeline / rooftops"
  • 45° – 75°: "Look high into the sky"
  • 75° – 90°: "Directly Overhead — look straight up!"

The gauge also displays real-time compass heading, slant range distance, and approach angle indicators so you can spot aircraft effortlessly.

What data is shown on the Aircraft Telemetry Card?

Selecting any flight in the airspace opens a comprehensive telemetry card containing:

  • Flight Identification: Operating carrier logo, flight callsign (e.g. DL142, UA882), and registered aircraft model (e.g. Airbus A350-900, Boeing 787-9 Dreamliner).
  • Route & Progress: Origin airport code (IATA/ICAO), destination airport code, distance remaining, and visual route progress bar.
  • Live Flight Dynamics: Real-time altitude (feet or meters), ground speed (knots or km/h), vertical ascent/descent rate, and true magnetic heading.
  • Spatial Proximity: Ground distance to your location, 3D slant range, and horizon elevation angle.

Geofencing & Smart Alerts

How does the Alert Radius (Geofence) work?

You can define an alert radius around your location (from 1 km / 1 mile up to 100+ km / 60+ miles). The app renders an interactive geofence boundary ring on the radar map.

You can configure whether this radius centers on:

  • Physical Device Location: Continuously follows your GPS coordinates as you move.
  • Pinned Map Center: Locks the radar and alert zone to any fixed coordinate (such as your home, office, an airport observation deck, or a vacation spot).
How do 24/7 Cloud Background Push Notifications work?

Unlike standard apps that stop updating when sent to the background or locked, Inbounder features server-backed Apple Push Notification (APNs) dispatch powered by the Intellirush Cloud Backend:

  1. When you enable background alerts, Inbounder securely syncs your active geofence center and radius to the Intellirush backend.
  2. The Intellirush backend monitors live airspace around your coordinates 24/7.
  3. When an inbound aircraft breaches your radius or meets your notification criteria, our cloud server instantly dispatches an APNs push notification directly to your iPhone, iPad, or Mac.
  4. You receive an alert with the airline, flight number, and altitude without keeping the app open or draining your battery.
⚡
Background push notifications require Inbounder Pro and Notification permissions enabled in iOS/macOS Settings.
What are the Notification Filter options?

To ensure you only receive the alerts you care about, Inbounder offers granular notification filters:

  • Radius Breach (Standard): Triggers an alert whenever any aircraft crosses inside your configured proximity circle.
  • Directly Overhead Only: Intelligently suppresses distant horizon traffic and alerts you only when an aircraft passes directly above you (steep elevation look-up angle > 65°).
  • Quiet Hours: Silence notifications during designated overnight periods.
  • Filter Suppression: If you have active aircraft filters (e.g. only widebody jets or cargo), notifications will automatically suppress any flights not matching your criteria.

Airspace Filters & Spotter Tools

How do Aircraft & Airspace Filters work?

Tap the Filter icon in the radar HUD to open multi-parameter filtering:

  • Aircraft Model: Filter by specific ICAO type codes (e.g. A388 for Airbus A380, B77W for Boeing 777-300ER, B738 for 737-800).
  • Carrier / Airline: Show only specific operators (e.g. FedEx, United, Delta, British Airways).
  • Airports (Origin / Destination): Filter flights departing from or arriving at specific airports by 3-letter IATA code (e.g. JFK, LHR, LAX).
  • Altitude Range: Filter by minimum and maximum cruising altitude (e.g., isolate low-altitude approaches below 5,000 ft or high-altitude overflights above 30,000 ft).
  • Special Squawk Codes: Highlight emergency transponder codes (7700 General Emergency, 7600 Radio Loss, 7500 Unlawful Interference).
Can I export spotter flight logs?

Yes! Aviation spotters can export aircraft observation logs directly from the app. You can export complete flight telemetry and observation histories in structured JSON or CSV formats to iCloud Drive, Files, or share via AirDrop.

Settings, macOS Features & Preferences

Can Inbounder run in the macOS Menu Bar and launch at startup?

Yes. On macOS, Inbounder can operate as a full desktop app or run discreetly in your Menu Bar:

  • Menu Bar Popover: Click the airplane icon in the menu bar to view active inbound flights, live distance readouts, and quick settings without occupying your Dock or main desktop.
  • Launch at Startup: Enable "Launch at Startup" in Settings so Inbounder automatically begins monitoring airspace whenever you boot your Mac.
What is the "Keep Screen On" (Screen Awake) feature?

The Keep Screen On setting overrides system display sleep timeouts. This prevents your iPhone, iPad, or Mac screen from dimming or sleeping while Inbounder is open — ideal when using your device as a dedicated tabletop radar display or cockpit side-monitor.

How does the Dynamic App Icon & Badge work?

On macOS and iOS, Inbounder dynamically adjusts its appearance:

  • Light & Dark Mode Adaptation: The icon automatically aligns with your system theme or selected preference (Light, Dark, or Accent).
  • Active Inbound Badge: Displays an active numerical badge or notification dot indicating the exact number of aircraft currently inside your alert zone.
Can I switch between Imperial and Metric units?

Yes. In the Settings sheet, toggle the Unit System between:

  • Imperial: Distances in Miles (mi), Altitudes in Feet (ft), and Speeds in Knots (kts) or MPH.
  • Metric: Distances in Kilometers (km), Altitudes in Meters (m), and Speeds in Kilometers per hour (km/h).

Troubleshooting & Diagnostics

Why am I not seeing any flights on the radar?

If the radar is empty or not showing aircraft:

  1. Check Auto Refresh: Verify that Tracking / Auto Refresh is toggled ON in the top status bar.
  2. Verify Location Permissions: Check that Location Services are set to "While Using the App" (or "Always" for background geofencing) in iOS/macOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > Inbounder.
  3. Check Active Airspace Filters: If you have model, airline, or altitude filters enabled, they may be hiding current flights. Tap the Filter button and tap "Reset Filters".
  4. Expand Alert Radius or Zoom Out: In rural or sparse airspace, there may simply be no aircraft directly within a small 5–10 km radius. Increase your radius to 30–50 km to inspect approaching traffic.
  5. Backend Status Indicator: In the status menu, verify that the Intellirush Cloud indicator is green (Operational).
Why am I not receiving background notifications on iOS?

To receive push alerts when the app is in the background or screen is locked:

  • Ensure Notifications are allowed for Inbounder in iOS Settings > Notifications > Inbounder > Allow Notifications (ensure Banners, Sounds, and Badges are turned on).
  • Verify that Background Alerts is enabled in Inbounder's Settings tab.
  • Confirm your device is connected to Wi-Fi or Cellular data so Apple's APNs service can deliver push payloads.
  • Check that Do Not Disturb or a Focus Mode is not silencing notifications.
